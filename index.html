My Favorite Place

Europe, France

Paris, the City of Lights

Paris is one of the most famous cities in the world. People travel there for the Eiffel Tower, the art, the old buildings, and the amazing food. I picked it as my featured destination because there is so much to do in one city, from museums to cafes to small shops on cobblestone streets.

Top Things to Do

  • Watch the sunset from the Eiffel Tower
  • See the Mona Lisa at the Louvre Museum
  • Take a boat ride down the Seine River
  • Walk around the artist area of Montmartre
Travel Tip: Buy your museum tickets online before you go so you can skip the long lines. Try to visit in April or October when the city is less crowded.

Top Destinations

These are five places around the world that I would love to travel to someday.

Paris

France

Paris is full of famous buildings, long history, and really good food.

Things to Do

  • Visit the Eiffel Tower
  • Explore the Louvre Museum
  • Walk along the Seine River
  • Eat French food at a cafe

Fun fact: The Eiffel Tower was only supposed to stand for 20 years. The city almost tore it down in 1909.

Banff

Canada

Banff is a mountain town with bright blue lakes and tons of outdoor activities to try.

Things to Do

  • Hike to Lake Louise
  • Drive the Icefields Parkway
  • Go canoeing on Moraine Lake
  • Look for bears and elk

Fun fact: Banff was Canada's very first national park. It was created all the way back in 1885.

Tokyo

Japan

Tokyo is a huge city where old temples and traditions mix with neon lights and new technology.

Things to Do

  • Visit the Sensoji Temple
  • Cross the famous Shibuya Crossing
  • Try real sushi and ramen
  • See the view from Tokyo Skytree

Fun fact: Tokyo has more Michelin star restaurants than any other city in the world.

Maui

Hawaii, USA

Maui has clean beaches, green mountains, and a relaxed island feel.

Things to Do

  • Relax on Kaanapali Beach
  • Watch the sunrise on Haleakala volcano
  • Go snorkeling or scuba diving
  • Drive the Road to Hana

Fun fact: Haleakala means "House of the Sun" in Hawaiian. The volcano is over 10,000 feet tall.

Santorini

Greece

Santorini has white buildings, blue roofs, and some of the best sunsets in the world.

Things to Do

  • Watch the sunset in Oia
  • Hike the trail along the cliffs
  • Swim at Red Beach
  • Visit the ancient ruins of Akrotiri

Fun fact: Santorini is actually the leftover rim of a giant volcano. The harbor sits inside the old crater.
