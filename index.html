Paris, the City of Lights

Paris is one of the most famous cities in the world. People travel there for the Eiffel Tower, the art, the old buildings, and the amazing food. There is so much to do, from museums to cafes to small shops on cobblestone streets.

Top Things to Do

  • Watch the sunset from the Eiffel Tower
  • See the Mona Lisa at the Louvre Museum
  • Take a boat ride down the Seine River
  • Walk around Montmartre
Travel Tip: Check ticket times before visiting popular museums and attractions.
